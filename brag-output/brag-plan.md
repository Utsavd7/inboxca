# Brag Plan: InboxCA

## What is this app?
InboxCA, a product by Orelu, is an email-first AI back office for Indian CA firms: it reads client emails, saves every paper in the right client folder in the firm's own drive, reminds clients only for what is still missing, and keeps forms ready for the team's approval.

## The angle
A CA's worst week, solved in 24 seconds. The video opens inside the problem every CA knows (filing week, an inbox that will not stop), then shows the product doing the four jobs in order on one fictional client, and lands on the brand line "Nothing pending." Reels-style: a POV hook that names the viewer, word-by-word captions timed to the voice (most feeds autoplay muted), a micro-demo instead of a feature list, and a counter that falls to zero as the payoff.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A pill reads "POV: you run a CA firm". Client emails slam into an inbox one per beat while the unread count races from 12 to 248. Caption, in time with the voice: "It's filing week." then "And your inbox just blew up."

## Key moments (the middle)
One client, one story (Patel Textiles, September GST; fictional sample data):
- Reads: a client email with an attachment is scanned and tagged with client, work type and period, then marked "Sorted".
- Saves: the attachment IMG_2291.pdf is renamed to the product's own naming rule and drops into `GST / FY 2026-27 / Patel Textiles`, "in your own OneDrive or Dropbox".
- Reminds: a three-line checklist shows two papers received and one missing; only the missing one gets "Reminder sent".
- Approves: "GSTR-3B · September, Ready for your approval"; a cursor clicks Approve and the button turns green.

## Outro / punchline
The unread count rolls from 248 down to 0 on a full blue panel: "Nothing pending." Then the logo, "InboxCA", "Less chasing. More doing.", "A product by Orelu".

## User flow worth showing
Email arrives → InboxCA reads and files it in the firm's drive → client is reminded only for the missing paper → the form is ready and the team approves. (The product is specified in PLAN.md and not yet built, so every screen is an illustration drawn from the plan and the brand kit, and is labelled "Illustration · sample data".)

## Tone
- Preset: default
- Creative direction: user asked for "best with voice and proper nice catchy with trends" → reels-style POV hook, kinetic captions synced to narration, quick micro-demo, big brand-line payoff.
- Interpretation: fast entrances and beat-timed cuts, but every caption holds while it is spoken; professional navy and white, no jokes at the CA's expense, no hype words.

## Audio direction
- Role: narration first, with a low rhythmic bed and sparse UI accents.
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10 (punchy, 110 BPM).
- Music treatment: starts at 0, sits at 0.15 under the voice for the whole video, fades out over the last second.
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read. Strong cues used: 18.55s ("Nothing pending." slam) and 20.74s (outro logo). Beat grid used: 0.27, 0.82, 1.37, 1.90, 2.46, 3.01 (hook emails), 4.10 (logo reveal), 6.82, 9.29, 12.56, 15.82 (step changes).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; the blue glow behind the content breathes with the bass. No waveform or equaliser graphics.
- SFX posture: moderate and quiet; soft drops for arriving emails, one click for the cursor, soft impacts for the reveal and the brand line, one bell for zero and one for the logo.
- Audio-coupled moments: hook emails on the beat, counter race, cursor click, counter reaching 0, logo landing.
- Restraint rule: nothing may mask a spoken word; no SFX louder than the voice.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1: Hook: the inbox blows up: 4.1s (0.0 to 4.1)
Navy with ledger lines. Pill "POV: you run a CA firm". Kinetic caption "It's filing week." then "And your inbox just blew up." An inbox card fills with six client emails; unread badge races 12 → 248; three more emails fall on top at an angle.
Sequential/interaction: yes. Six email rows arrive one per beat; the rows are atmosphere, the caption is the read.
Audio intent: mounting pressure.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft drop per email, on the beat.
Music: punchy bed, low.
Transition mood: hard, everything is swept away → Scene 2

### Scene 2: Reveal: Meet InboxCA: 2.7s (4.1 to 6.82)
Logo tile lands on the beat, the green tick arrives from outside and lands in the tray. "Meet" then "InboxCA". Line: "AI back office for CA firms".
Sequential/interaction: tick draws in.
Audio intent: relief, one clean hit.
Audio-coupled idea: soft impact as the tile lands.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3: The flow in four steps: 11.6s (6.82 to 18.45)
A white InboxCA window stays on screen; a step counter runs 01 to 04; the caption above changes with the voice.
- 01 Reads (6.82 to 9.29): email from Patel Textiles is scanned, tagged, "Sorted".
- 02 Saves (9.29 to 12.56): IMG_2291.pdf becomes "Patel Textiles - Purchase bills - Sep 2026 - 10 Oct 2026.pdf" and drops into GST / FY 2026-27 / Patel Textiles; "In your own OneDrive or Dropbox".
- 03 Reminds (12.56 to 15.82): Sales register received, Purchase bills received, Bank statement missing → "Reminder sent".
- 04 Approves (15.82 to 18.45): "GSTR-3B · September", "Ready for your approval"; cursor clicks Approve; button turns green "Approved".
Sequential/interaction: yes. Tags, folder levels and checklist rows arrive in order; a simulated cursor click.
Audio intent: calm competence, small confirmations.
Audio-coupled idea: click on the cursor press; soft drop when the file lands and when the reminder goes.
Transition mood: hard → Scene 4

### Scene 4: Payoff: Nothing pending: 2.2s (18.45 to 20.65)
Full blue panel. Mono unread counter rolls 248 → 0, a green done tick lands beside it. "Nothing pending." fills the frame.
Sequential/interaction: counter roll-down.
Audio intent: the release.
Audio-coupled idea: heavy soft impact on the strong cue, bell at zero.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 5

### Scene 5: Outro: 3.85s (20.65 to 24.5)
Navy. Logo, "InboxCA", "Less chasing. More doing.", "For Indian CA firms", "A product by Orelu".
Sequential/interaction: tick draws in; tagline lines arrive with the voice.
Audio intent: sign-off.
Audio-coupled idea: bell on the logo, music fades.
Transition mood: end.
